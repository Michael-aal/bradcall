BRADCALL POLICY SET · DRAFT v1

Terms, payments & trust policies

Original working policies describing intended Bradcall rules. A qualified Nigerian lawyer must review these before launch, and final terms must match Paystack's approved configuration and actual app behaviour.

Important: Bradcall is a marketplace connecting customers and independent providers. A payment receipt is not a guarantee of service quality. Bradcall does not claim to hold money in escrow unless a compliant, approved payment arrangement actually supports it.

1. Terms of Service

Users must provide accurate details, protect credentials, communicate respectfully, and use Bradcall lawfully. Before a job begins, both sides should agree the scope, price, location, appointment, cancellation terms and payment method. Providers are responsible for the services they offer and must not misrepresent qualifications or completed work. Impersonation, fabricated evidence, fake payment confirmations, harassment and unlawful use are prohibited.

Bradcall may investigate reports, request information, issue warnings, restrict features, suspend or close accounts when reasonably justified, and retain records needed for security and dispute resolution. Affected users should receive reasons and a reasonable appeal path, except where temporary protective action is needed urgently.

2. Payment & Commission Policy

Paystack processes enabled online checkout. Bradcall verifies reference, amount, currency and status on the server before recording payment as confirmed. A redirect, screenshot or claim alone is not proof. Users must never share card PINs, passwords or one-time passwords. Provider commissions and processing fees must be clearly disclosed before acceptance.

Cash is not a Paystack transaction. A provider reports the amount received and the customer may confirm or dispute the report. This creates an audit trail, not independent proof that cash changed hands. Cash commission is tracked separately under the provider agreement and is not automatically deducted from cash. Ordinary split settlement is not escrow or a promise of delayed payout.

3. Cancellation, No-Show & Refund Policy

Appointments should be recorded and confirmed. If either party does not arrive, the other may open a dispute after the stated grace period and submit relevant messages, receipts and appointment details. Both sides can respond. Admins review conflicting claims fairly.

Refunds depend on the payment method, transaction status, facts, disclosed terms and gateway capabilities. Bradcall may initiate a Paystack refund only where supported and authorised; it is complete only when the provider confirms it. Cash refunds must be arranged with the party who received the cash and recorded. Bradcall cannot promise recovery of money already settled directly to a provider. Statutory rights remain unaffected.

4. Service Provider Agreement

Providers must keep listings accurate, respond promptly, attend confirmed appointments or communicate changes, deliver agreed work, and record completion honestly. Price or scope changes require customer approval. Providers must not falsely report cash, evade disclosed platform fees, fabricate reviews or misrepresent identity. Verification means only that Bradcall completed the checks described to users; it is not a guarantee of conduct or work quality.

Providers agree to clearly disclosed commission rules for eligible jobs. Disputed commissions can be reviewed. Restrictions should be proportionate, documented and appealable.

5. Privacy Policy

Bradcall collects account information, listings, requests, messages, support reports, payment references/statuses, policy acceptance records and moderation history. It uses this information to operate the marketplace, support transactions, prevent abuse, resolve disputes and comply with legal obligations. Card details should be handled by Paystack, not stored in Bradcall's app database.

Information is shared only as needed with service counterparties, payment processors, hosting suppliers, authorised support staff and lawful authorities. If AI-assisted moderation is enabled, report text may be sent to the configured AI provider for triage; unnecessary sensitive details should be avoided. Access to private messages and reports is role-restricted. Before launch, Bradcall must publish a contact address, retention periods, data-request procedures and an incident-response process.

6. Trust & Safety and AI

Automated rules or AI may summarise reports and flag patterns for human review. Automated flags can be wrong and are not proof. Serious sanctions require human review of relevant evidence and the affected person's response where practicable. Decisions should record the reason, timestamp and responsible admin. Users may appeal an action.

7. Complaints

Customers and providers can create a support ticket in Bradcall's Trust Centre and link a service request. A monitored support contact and response-time commitment must be configured before public launch.
